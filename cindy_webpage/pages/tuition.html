Tuition, Rules & Important Dates

A simple guide to registration, monthly tuition, recital fees, attendance policies, and the 2025–2026 studio calendar.

Tuition Due

Monthly tuition is due on the 1st of each month.

Late Fee

A $10 late fee applies after the 10th.

Non-Refundable

Registration, tuition, and costume payments are non-refundable.

Payment & Studio Policies

These policies help the studio keep classes, costumes, and recital planning organized for every family.

Tuition Payments

Monthly tuition is due on the 1st of each month. The studio does not send monthly bills.

  • $10 late fee after the 10th
  • $10 fee for returned or failed payments
  • June tuition is prorated to half of a month

Withdrawal Notice

Families are responsible for all enrolled classes until Cindy's Dance Studio receives written notice of withdrawal.

Payment Methods

The studio accepts cash, check, money order, cashier's check, Visa, and MasterCard for tuition payments.

Auto Pay

CDS Auto Pay is available for tuition payments with a $3 convenience fee.

Non-refundable policy: All registration and tuition fees are non-refundable.

Recital & Costume Fees

Costume and recital payments are handled separately from monthly tuition.

Costume Balance Due
November, before Thanksgiving Break.

Recital Participation Fee
$25 per family to help cover recital T-shirts, programs, and theater rental.

Important: Costume payments are not on Auto Pay and are non-refundable. Accounts must be current to receive recital costumes and participate in the annual recital. Costume payments should be made separately from tuition.

Attendance & Make-Up Classes

The studio follows the Seminole County Public School session, with the listed studio closures.

Absences

If a student is absent, they may attend a comparable make-up class at a later date agreed upon by the parent and instructor.

Closures

Holidays, missed classes, and severe weather closures may be made up based on availability.

Make-Up Responsibility

Scheduling make-up classes is the parent's responsibility.

No Tuition Adjustments

Tuition is not adjusted for holidays, missed classes, or classes that are not made up.

Notices for schedule changes and holiday breaks will be posted in the studio.
